03 / Local by design

Your words are yours.
Let’s keep it that way.

A thought does not need to travel to a server to become text. Utterlane keeps speech recognition where it belongs: on your device.

Read the privacy policy

No speech uploads.

Your audio and transcripts are not sent to a speech service. Recognition runs locally, even in airplane mode.

No account. No analytics.

There is nothing to sign up for. Optional microphone history is off by default, and you control what you keep or export.

Open source. Open to scrutiny.

The code is available under Apache-2.0. Read it, build it, improve it. Good tools should be accessible to everyone.

FAQ

Ask on GitHub
Does it really work offline?

Yes. Once a speech model is installed, recognition works without a network connection. The initial model download uses Hugging Face; you can also import model files from a local folder.

What does “near real-time” mean?

Completed text segments arrive while microphone capture continues. This is not instantaneous word-by-word output. Timing depends on your phone, the selected model, and pauses in your speech.

The default Parakeet v3 backend simulates streaming over an offline model. Read how streaming works.

Can I dictate into other apps?

Use a compatible keyboard’s microphone button, the accessibility button, or the floating mic. Utterlane inserts text into the focused field and can fall back to clipboard or transcript recovery when insertion is unavailable.

Integration depends on the receiving app and keyboard. HeliBoard is one compatible keyboard; AOSP Keyboard does not offer this voice-input integration.

Which languages and audio files are supported?

Utterlane automatically detects 25 recognition languages: Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, and Ukrainian.

Share or open OPUS, AAC, OGG, M4A, MP3, and WAV files, subject to your device’s codec support. You can also monitor selected folders for newly saved audio.

What happens to my recordings?

Microphone history is disabled by default. Enable it if you want to replay or retranscribe recordings, with configurable retention. Saved history is private to the app and excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Temporary text files support long transcripts and recovery. When you choose to copy, share, or export, the receiving app has its own privacy behavior.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account or subscription is required. Utterlane is open source under Apache-2.0; models and third-party libraries retain their own licenses.

If it helps you, help it grow: report a bug, improve a translation, or contribute code. Contributions are welcome.
