Utterlane — fast, offline transcription for Android. Speak into your phone and receive text as you go, with speech recognition running entirely on your device. No account or subscription required.

<b>Features</b>

<b>Offline Speech Recognition</b>
Uses NVIDIA Parakeet TDT v3 via sherpa-onnx, with optional Moondream Parakeet Ultra/Redux models through CrispASR. Supports 25 languages with auto-detection. Download a model or import its files locally; recognition then works without internet.

<b>Incremental Results</b>
Completed text segments appear while you continue speaking. Audio-file transcription also shows partial results. Output arrives in segments, with latency depending on the selected model and device.

<b>Works in Any App</b>
Uses Android Accessibility API to inject text directly into any focused text field. Falls back to clipboard if no text field is focused.

<b>Multiple Input Methods</b>
• Accessibility Button — System navigation bar button, works system-wide
• Floating Mic Button — Draggable overlay, always visible
• Keyboard Integration — Works with keyboards like HeliBoard

<b>Voice Message Transcription</b>
Share or open audio files from any app to transcribe them. Supports OPUS, AAC, OGG, M4A, MP3, WAV formats. Optional folder monitoring watches your Downloads folder for new voice messages.

<b>Word Corrections</b>
Custom dictionary to fix recurring recognition errors. Define rules like "Tamtam" → "Tamdam" and they're applied automatically.

<b>Privacy First</b>
All speech recognition happens locally. No audio is uploaded to a speech service, and there are no analytics or tracking. Microphone history defaults to one-hour local retention for replay and retranscription; you can disable it or change retention, and existing choices are preserved. Explicitly sharing audio or text gives it to your chosen receiving app.

<b>Accessibility</b>
This app uses Android's Accessibility Service to help users who have difficulty typing on touchscreens. Voice input is directly inserted into any text field, making the device more accessible for users with motor impairments or other conditions that make typing challenging.

<b>Supported Languages</b>
English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Slovenian, Greek, Danish, Swedish, Finnish, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese.

<b>Permissions</b>
• Microphone — to capture voice for transcription
• Accessibility — to inject text into apps
• Overlay — for floating mic button
• Storage — to access audio files for transcription
• Internet — requested model downloads
