# Settings submenus and speaker recovery

Replace the long Settings list with a short directory: Speech recognition, Speaker labels (diarization), Voice input, Audio files & folders, History & storage, and Appearance & language. Move existing controls intact to their logical page. Keep onboarding replay and the privacy link on the directory. Use real navigable pages rather than accordion sections: pages give a stable in-app destination for recovery, retain sensible Back behavior, and make the download action discoverable without scrolling through unrelated controls.

When labels require a missing speaker model, expose the dedicated page from the affected recording or file UI. On the keyboard and other shared recording panels, an explicitly labeled **Finish recording and open speaker settings** action delivers the result before leaving the editor; the warning itself does not stop or navigate. In file setup and the Home workspace the shortcut can open Settings directly without discarding the active input. Hide the Home tabs on direct recovery pages so Back returns to the actual caller. Do not require a model for the fixed one-speaker mode, download automatically, discard selected audio, change unrelated preferences, or change the existing plain-transcription fallback. Keep the existing speech-model recovery destination and model-selector behavior.
